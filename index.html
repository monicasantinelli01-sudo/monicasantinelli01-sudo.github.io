Prostitution in San Francisco

On the following website, Prostitution as a crime category was analyzed in San Francisco. The data sources for the subsequent visuals are two official datasets from the San Francisco Police Department (SFPD). One covers the time from 2003 to 2018 and the other is covering the period from 2018 until the end of 2025. The datasets were merged and our group aligned on specifically elaborating how the crime patterns of Prostitution changed within the above-mentioned time period.
